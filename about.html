About us

Maths taught by someone who loves it

Full Marks Maths was set up to help A Level students stop dreading maths and start enjoying it, and to get the grades they're capable of.

Our approach

Most students don't struggle because they're "bad at maths". They struggle because a gap from earlier on was never filled, or because nobody showed them how exam questions really work.

Every lesson is one-to-one, so we can find exactly where things went wrong, rebuild understanding from there, and then practise until it feels natural.

We teach the reasoning behind each method, then how to present it so the marks follow.
